# Code of conduct

Reader: everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Last reviewed: Batch 1, 5 October 2026

This project deals with a painful subject. People who join may have been hurt by it. We ask everyone to be kind, careful, and clear.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Assume good faith, and ask when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Say what you do not know. Do not round a guess up to a fact.
- Welcome beginners. Everyone here is learning something.
- Keep every discussion free of images of people, and of graphic descriptions.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information about someone.
- Requests for undressing, face-swap, or similar abuse features. See [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md).
- Sexual content or sexual jokes.
- Posting images of people, in any form.

## What happens when someone breaks these rules

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block a person from the project. The maintainer will explain the decision when it is safe to do so.

## How to report a problem

Email nothingblank37@gmail.com. Say what happened and where. Your report stays private. We will not share your name without asking you.

If the report is about abusive material involving a child, do not send us the material. Follow rule 6 in [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md).

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces. It also applies when someone speaks for the project in public.
