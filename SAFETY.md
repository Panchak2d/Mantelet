# Safety rules

Reader: every contributor and reviewer. Also anyone who wants to know what this project will never do.

Last reviewed: 5 October 2026

These rules have no exceptions. Reviewers check every change against them. If a rule blocks something you want to do, change the plan, not the rule.

## 1. No sexual or nude image generation

No code, prompt, test, dataset, or output in this repository creates or edits images into nude or sexual images of any real or realistic person.

We test with harmless stand-in edits instead. Examples are changing the colour of a shirt, or filling in a background patch.

## 2. No images of minors

No image of a person under 18 appears in the repository, the tests, the datasets, the issues, or the pull requests. This holds even with permission.

Because of this, any claim about children is reasoned from tests on adults and on synthetic faces. Every document that makes such a claim says so.

## 3. No real people without consent

Test images are synthetic faces, or adult volunteers who gave written consent. Consent records are kept outside the repository.

## 4. Licences first

Every dataset and model has its licence recorded before anyone uses it. Some face models are for research only. We check each one.

## 5. No abuse features

We close, without discussion, any contribution that adds undressing, face-swap, or similar abuse features.

## 6. If you find abusive material involving a child

If you find it in an issue, a pull request, or a dataset:

- Do not share it or copy it.
- Report it to your national hotline, or to the NCMEC CyberTipline (see [missingkids.org](https://www.missingkids.org/)).
- Tell the maintainer privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how.

## 7. Attack code is for testing protections

The red team tools attack our own protections, using methods that are already public. They do not target people.

## How these rules are enforced

- The pull request template asks the author to confirm that no images of people or abusive content were added, and that every dataset and model has a recorded licence.
- Issue forms ask the reporter to confirm that no images of people are attached.
- Reviewers check each change against this page before it is merged.

Nothing here can stop someone who ignores the rules. It is a promise we make and check, and not a technical lock.
