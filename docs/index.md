# Mantelet documentation

Mantelet is an open research project. It asks whether it can be made much harder to turn an ordinary photo of a woman or a child into a fake nude image. It does not protect anyone yet. Read [Limits](limits.md) before anything else if you are thinking of relying on it.

This index lists every page and says who it is for.

## If you were targeted, or you care for someone who was

1. [Get help](get-help.md): steps to take today, for adults, under 18s, parents and schools, with helplines and the law by country.
2. [Background](background.md): the problem, with sourced facts and what each number measures.
3. [Limits](limits.md): what this project cannot do.
4. [Glossary](glossary.md): plain meanings for technical words.
