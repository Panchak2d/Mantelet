Last reviewed: 5 October 2026

Every source in this list was checked against the real world. The links and dates are correct. The full list is in the [bibliography](../../research/bibliography.md).

## Adversarial machine learning 

Read in this order. Find each one on arxiv.org by title, or use the links in the [bibliography](../../research/bibliography.md).

1. Goodfellow, Shlens, Szegedy, "Explaining and Harnessing Adversarial Examples" (2014)
2. Madry et al., "Towards Deep Learning Models Resistant to Adversarial Attacks" (2017)
3. Athalye, Engstrom, Ilyas, Kwok, "Synthesizing Robust Adversarial Examples" (2017)
4. Athalye, Carlini, Wagner, "Obfuscated Gradients Give a False Sense of Security: Circumventing Defenses to Adversarial Examples" (2018)
5. Carlini et al., "On Evaluating Adversarial Robustness" (2019)
6. Tramer et al., "On Adaptive Attacks to Adversarial Example Defenses" (2020)

## People, ethics, and law (before steps 7 and 8)

| Topic | Where |
|---|---|
| Research ethics and consent | The Belmont Report. Your own institution's or country's rules for studies with volunteers |
| US takedown law | The TAKE IT DOWN Act and the FTC business guidance on complying with it (ftc.gov) |
| Hash-based takedown services | NCMEC Take It Down for images of people under 18. StopNCII.org for adults |
| Other countries | A research task. Start with official government and hotline sites, not blogs |
| Statistics for honest reporting | "Statistics Done Wrong" (statisticsdonewrong.com) or the free Khan Academy statistics course |
