# Start here

Reader: a parent, teacher, or victim with no technical background.

Last reviewed: Batch 1, 5 October 2026

This page tells you what Mantelet is, who it is for, and why it exists. It also tells you what it cannot do.

## What is the problem?

Some computer programs can change a photo of a real person. They can make it look like the person has no clothes on. The picture is fake. The harm to the real person is not fake.

Anyone can find a photo of a woman or a child online. That is why this problem is hard.

## What is Mantelet?

Mantelet is a research project. We are trying to find out one thing:

Can we make a photo harder to misuse, by making small changes to it that people cannot see?

Think of it like a lock on a door. We do not know yet if the lock works. We also do not know if a person who really wants to get in can pick it. Our job is to find out, and to tell the truth about it.

## Does Mantelet protect anyone today?

No. Nothing is built yet. There is no app, no website, and no tool you can use.

If we build something later, it may still fail. A photo can be copied before any protection is added. One unprotected photo of the same person is enough to get around it. We explain more on the [Limits](limits.md) page.

## Who is this for?

- Parents and teachers who want honest answers.
- People who have been hurt by a fake image, or who know someone who has.
- People who want to learn how this kind of research works.
- Researchers who want to check our work.

## What can you do today?

The research may take a long time. Some help does not need to wait for it. We are writing guides that cover how to ask for an image to be removed, and where to report it. The first draft is on the page [For families](for-families.md). It is short for now, and we say plainly what is missing.

## Why should you trust us?

You should not trust us because we say so. Here is what we do instead:

- We write down every claim in a public list, with a label that says how we know it.
- We publish failed experiments, not only the ones that worked.
- We never use images of children. We never make fake nude images of anyone, even for testing.
- We say what we do not know.

The hard safety rules are in [SAFETY.md](../SAFETY.md).
